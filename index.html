Install in 60 seconds

Download once. Updates itself forever.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later on Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4). Runs silently as a lightweight menu bar utility with no Dock icon.

  1. 01

    Download & Drag

    Grab KramaIsland.dmg (3.3 MB) and drag KramaIsland into your Applications folder.

  2. 02

    Confirm First Launch

    Because KramaIsland is free, open source, and signed with its own local certificate rather than a paid Apple Developer ID, confirm launch once in macOS Gatekeeper (see guide below).

  3. 03

    Grant Permissions On Demand

    Open Settings ▸ Privacy for one-click status checks: Accessibility, Full Disk Access, plus optional Calendar, Camera, Bluetooth & Automation.

  4. 04

    Automatic In-Place Updates

    That's your last DMG download. KramaIsland checks GitHub Releases daily (toggleable offline) and installs signature-verified updates in place while preserving every permission.

First-launch Gatekeeper guide

Choose your macOS version or run the one-line Terminal command to clear the download quarantine flag.

When macOS says "Apple could not verify KramaIsland is free of malware", click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "KramaIsland was blocked", and click Open Anyway.

Open your Applications folder, right-click KramaIsland.app, choose Open, and confirm Open in the dialog. You only ever need to do this once.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/KramaIsland.app

Questions & Answers

Everything you might wonder.

Does KramaIsland work on external monitors or non-notch Macs?

Yes. On notched MacBooks it measures the real physical notch from NSScreen with smooth concave top shoulders. On external monitors or non-notch displays, it renders a sleek unified compact Dynamic Island pill at the top center — and you can pin it to Auto, Main, or any specific connected display in Settings.

Does KramaIsland send any notifications, clipboard items, or analytics to a server?

Never. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no backend servers. Everything is processed strictly on your Mac. Clipboard history stays inside your current boot session and auto-wipes on shutdown/restart. The only optional network request is a daily check against GitHub Releases for app updates, which you can disable with Strict Offline Mode.

Allowed a permission in System Settings, but KramaIsland says it's missing?

macOS binds TCC permissions to the cryptographic signature of the specific app build. Since v1.1.1, KramaIsland automatically detects stale grants after major signature changes and offers a one-click Reset… button right inside Settings ▸ Permissions so you can re-grant cleanly.

How do I enable full per-tab media controls in Chrome, Arc, Brave, or Safari?

Every browser already works out of the box via the system Now Playing feed with zero permission required. To unlock per-tab source chips and one-click tab jumping, enable View ▸ Developer ▸ Allow JavaScript from Apple Events in Chromium browsers, or Develop ▸ Allow JavaScript from Apple Events in Safari.
